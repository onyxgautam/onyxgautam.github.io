My long walks

I like taking long walks.

My longest walk was 34 miles throughout Manhattan and Brooklyn. It included the length of Manhattan tip-to-tip. The northern tip, in Inwood Hill Park, was surprisingly hard to find (my hands even got a little dirty!), and I was disappointed to see that there was no plaque commemorating its location.

My most recent walk went from Hudson Yards to Manhasset. It was around 25 miles. The original goal was the "7 train walk," from Hudson Yards to Flushing. There was plenty of time left in the day when we got to Flushing, so we decided to extend it Port Washington, covering the rest of the nearest LIRR line. We ran out of sidewalk (and sunlight) in the affluent village of Plandome, shortly after passing Manhasset. Since there was an LIRR stop in Manhasset, we decided to turn back and call that the end of our walk. My favourite part of this walk was the progression from city to suburb. We saw midtown skyscrapers, then Queens apartment buildings, then multi-family homes, then single family homes, and finally Long Island mansions.
